# A07 – Health Care

> [!abstract] Zadanie
> Talk about the human body and common illnesses, a visit to the doctor,
> health care in Slovakia, and prevention and a healthy lifestyle.

## 1. Osnova témy

- Parts of the human body.
- Common illnesses, their symptoms and treatment.
- Serious and civilisation diseases.
- A visit to the doctor — from making an appointment to the prescription.
- Health care in Slovakia — state and private facilities, specialists, emergencies.
- A healthy lifestyle and prevention.
- Alternative medicine and your own opinion of it.

## 2. Kľúčová slovná zásoba

- **parts of the body:** head, forehead (čelo), cheek (líce), chin (brada), neck, throat (hrdlo), shoulder, chest (hruď), back, arm, elbow (lakeť), wrist (zápästie), palm (dlaň), thumb (palec), waist (pás), hip (bok), thigh (stehno), knee, calf (lýtko), ankle (členok), foot, toe (prst na nohe), skin, bone (kosť)
- **common illnesses:** flu (chrípka), a cold (nádcha), headache, earache, stomachache, backache, toothache, tonsillitis (angína), bronchitis, pneumonia (zápal pľúc)
- **contagious diseases:** chicken pox (ovčie kiahne), measles (osýpky), mumps (mumps), jaundice (žltačka), scarlet fever (šarlach)
- **civilisation diseases:** asthma, diabetes (cukrovka), allergies, migraine, hypertension (vysoký tlak), heart attack (infarkt), stroke (porážka), ulcer (vred), obesity
- **serious diseases:** cancer (rakovina), tumour (nádor), leukaemia, AIDS, osteoporosis, Alzheimer's disease
- **symptoms:** high temperature, fever (horúčka), cough (kašeľ), sore throat, runny nose, sneezing (kýchanie), rash (vyrážka), loss of appetite, diarrhoea (hnačka), vomiting (zvracanie), nausea (nevoľnosť), cramps (kŕče), insomnia (nespavosť), sweating
- **injuries:** bruise (modrina), blister (pľuzgier), burn (popálenina), cut, fracture (zlomenina); broken, swollen (opuchnutý), sprained (vyvrtnutý), bleeding (krvácajúci)
- **doctors:** GP — general practitioner (obvodný lekár), dentist, paediatrician (detský lekár), surgeon (chirurg), ophthalmologist (očný), dermatologist (kožný), cardiologist, psychologist, physiotherapist
- **what a doctor does:** to examine a patient (vyšetriť), to listen to someone's breathing, to take a blood count (odobrať krv), to measure blood pressure, to prescribe medicine (predpísať lieky), to operate, to X-ray
- **medicaments:** painkillers (lieky proti bolesti), antibiotics, pills, tablets, capsule, syrup, drops (kvapky), ointment (masť), injection, vaccine (vakcína), dosage (dávkovanie), side effects (vedľajšie účinky), prescription (recept), pharmacy / chemist's (lekáreň)
- **medical facilities:** hospital, ward (oddelenie), surgery / doctor's office (ambulancia), waiting room (čakáreň), clinic, spa (kúpele), maternity hospital (pôrodnica), nursing home, hospice, ambulance (sanitka)
- **healthy lifestyle:** balanced diet, wholesome food, lean meat (chudé mäso), regular exercise, sufficient sleep, preventive medical check-up (preventívna prehliadka), vaccination (očkovanie), to avoid smoking and stress
- **alternative medicine:** acupuncture (akupunktúra), homeopathy, aromatherapy, acupressure

## 4. Modelová odpoveď

Health is probably the most valuable thing we have, and unfortunately we usually only realise it when we lose it. That is why everybody should look after their health.

The most common illnesses are the flu and a cold. When you have the flu, you run a high temperature, your whole body aches and you feel weak. You should see your doctor, stay in bed, drink a lot of hot tea and take the medicine he prescribes. With a cold you sneeze, your nose is running and you have a sore throat, but you usually get over it in a few days. Other common problems are a headache, a toothache, tonsillitis or a stomachache.

There are also far more serious diseases. Cancer is one of them — the patient has a tumour and is treated with medicine, radiation or chemotherapy. A heart attack and a stroke are among the most frequent causes of death. AIDS destroys the immune system, so that even a small infection can be fatal. We also talk about so-called civilisation diseases, such as asthma, allergies, diabetes, high blood pressure and migraines. They are caused by stress, a hectic lifestyle, insufficient sleep, a polluted environment, sedentary work and overeating.

When I feel ill, I go to see my GP. First I have to make an appointment and I must take my insurance card with me. In the surgery the doctor asks me how I feel and what my problems are, then he examines me — he listens to my breathing, looks at my throat and measures my blood pressure. Finally he prescribes some medicine, which I collect at the chemist's. Sometimes he sends me to a specialist, for example to a dentist, a surgeon or a dermatologist.

Health care in Slovakia is provided from birth to death and there are both state and private facilities. Medical care is given in health centres, clinics, hospitals and private surgeries. A paediatrician looks after children, who are vaccinated against diseases such as tuberculosis and tetanus soon after birth, while adults are looked after by a general practitioner. In case of emergency you call an ambulance, and serious cases are operated on immediately in the operating theatre. If you break an arm or a leg, you are X-rayed and the limb is put in plaster.

The best treatment, however, is prevention. A healthy lifestyle means regular exercise, plenty of rest and enough sleep, good eating habits with a lot of fruit and vegetables, and no smoking or alcohol. We should also go for preventive check-ups, visit the dentist twice a year and try to think positively. It is better to get vitamins from fresh raw fruit and vegetables than from pills, because cooking destroys a lot of the vitamins.

Some people also use alternative medicine, such as acupuncture, homeopathy or aromatherapy. Personally I think it may help some people to relax, but for a serious illness I would always trust a proper doctor.

## 6. Zhrnutie na ústnu odpoveď

- **Bežné choroby:** flu (temperature, aching body) · a cold (sneezing, runny nose, sore throat) — always say the symptoms *and* the treatment.
- **Vážne a civilizačné:** cancer, heart attack, stroke, AIDS · asthma, diabetes, allergies, hypertension; causes = stress, poor diet, no exercise, pollution.
- **U lekára:** make an appointment → insurance card → the doctor examines you → prescribes medicine → chemist's → specialist if needed.
- **Zdravotníctvo v SR:** state and private · health centre, clinic, hospital, surgery · paediatrician vs. GP · vaccination · ambulance in an emergency.
- **Prevencia:** exercise, sleep, diet, no smoking, check-ups, dentist twice a year.
- **Alternatívna medicína:** acupuncture, homeopathy — and your own opinion.
