> [!abstract] O zozname
> 30 tém, zostavené z materiálov v `99 Zdroje/` — z učiteľkiných
> `.doc` okruhov a z naskenovanej učebnice *ANGLIČTINA – MATURITA*.

> [!warning] Tento zoznam nevydala predmetová komisia
> Oficiálne maturitné témy z angličtiny **stále nemáš** — škola ich
> vydáva sama a v trezore po nich nie je stopa. Tento zoznam je
> zrekonštruovaný zo študijných materiálov, ktoré si dostal, takže
> pokrýva to, čo sa preberalo, ale číslovanie ani presné znenie
> nemusia sedieť. **Over si ho u vyučujúcej.**

## Úplné znenie zadaní

**A01** — Talk about your family and its members, their appearance and character, household duties, the leisure time you spend together, and the relationship between parents and children.

**A02** — Talk about the branches of art, the cultural events you attend, theatre, cinema and museums, and your own attitude to culture.

**A03** — Talk about sports and games, summer and winter sports, sport at school, professional and amateur sport, and your own relationship to sport.

**A04** — Talk about where you live, describe your house or flat and your room, compare housing in towns and in the countryside, and describe your ideal home.

**A05** — Talk about your eating habits, healthy eating, the food you like and dislike, fast food, diets and vegetarians, and food around the world.

**A06** — Talk about the types of shops and the way people shop, the services people use, paying and complaining, advertising, and your own shopping habits.

**A07** — Talk about the human body and common illnesses, a visit to the doctor, health care in Slovakia, and prevention and a healthy lifestyle.

**A08** — Talk about the school system in Slovakia, your school and your subjects, a typical school day, exams, and your plans for further study.

**A09** — Talk about jobs and professions, choosing a career, applying for a job, working conditions and unemployment, and your own plans for the future.

**A10** — Talk about relationships between people, friendship and love, relationships in the family and at school, and conflicts and the ways of solving them.

**A11** — Talk about the seasons of the year, the most serious ecological problems, the protection of the environment, and the organizations that protect nature.

**A12** — Talk about the inventions of the past and of today, the technology we use every day, famous inventors and scientists, and the advantages and disadvantages that science and technology brought to mankind.

**A13** — Talk about the forms of communication, verbal and non-verbal communication, the modern means of communication, and their advantages and disadvantages.

**A14** — Talk about why people learn foreign languages, how languages are taught and learned, the world languages, and your own experience with English.

**A15** — Talk about the position of the individual in society, social groups and social problems, tolerance and prejudice, and the role of the state and of volunteering.

**A16** — Talk about the kinds of mass media, television, the press, radio and the internet, their influence on people, and your own use of the media.

**A17** — Talk about the characteristic features of young people, their appearance, lifestyle and interests, the advantages and disadvantages of being young, and the problems young people face.

**A18** — Talk about men's and women's fashion, your own wardrobe, your attitude to fashion, and how the weather and the occasion influence what people wear.

**A19** — Talk about leisure activities in general, your own free time, the after-school activities at your school, and men's and women's hobbies.

**A20** — Talk about the reasons why people travel, the means of transport and their advantages and disadvantages, travelling on holiday, and your own travel experience.

**A21** — Talk about different places and the people who live there, your favourite place, places to spend a holiday, and towns and places in Slovakia.

**A22** — Talk about what a multicultural society is, immigration and cultural adaptation, its advantages and disadvantages, and tolerance towards other cultures.

**A23** — Talk about the holidays and celebrations in Slovakia, the holidays in English-speaking countries, and family celebrations.

**A24** — Talk about reading and its importance, the kinds of books and literary genres, your favourite book and author, and libraries and e-books.

**A25** — Talk about examples and ideals, who young people admire and why, the difference between an idol and a role model, and your own example.

**A26** — Talk about the general information on the United Kingdom, its location, population, industry and educational system, the interesting places in Britain, London as the capital city, famous British people, and customs and traditions.

**A27** — Talk about the general information on the USA, its geography, climate, industry and political system, the history of the country, the places of interest, and Washington D.C. as the capital city.

**A28** — Talk about the general information on Canada, its location, geography, climate, industry and political system, the places of interest, Ottawa as the capital city, and Canadian people, their customs and traditions.

**A29** — Talk about the general information on Australia and New Zealand, and the places of interest in both countries.

**A30** — Talk about the general information on Slovakia, its geography, climate, industry and political system, Bratislava as the capital city, the places of interest, and Slovak customs and traditions.
