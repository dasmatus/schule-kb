# All About Circuits – Lessons in Electric Circuits

> [!info] Originál
> [allaboutcircuits.com/textbook](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/) — obsah prevzatý november 2026, pozri pôvodný web pre aktuálne znenie.

Bezplatná viaczväzková učebnica elektrotechniky (Tony R. Kuphaldt, licencovaná Design Science License; prerobená portálom All About Circuits).

## Obsah (zväzky a kapitoly, november 2026)

- [Direct Current (DC)](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/direct-current/) — 16 kapitol: Ohm, sériovo-paralelné obvody, Kirchhoffove zákony, kondenzátory, induktory, RC/L/R časové konštanty
- [Alternating Current (AC)](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/alternating-current/) — 14 kapitol: reaktancia a impedancia R/L/C, rezonancia, filtre, [transformátory](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/alternating-current/chpt-9/mutual-inductance-and-basic-operation/), trojfázové systémy
- [Semiconductors](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/semiconductors/) — 12 kapít:
  1. Amplifiers and Active Devices
  2. Solid-state Device Theory ([band theory](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/semiconductors/chpt-2/band-theory-of-solids/), [electrons and holes](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/semiconductors/chpt-2/electrons-and-holes/), [P-N junction](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/semiconductors/chpt-2/the-p-n-junction/))
  3. Diodes and Rectifiers ([rectifier circuits](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/semiconductors/chpt-3/rectifier-circuits/), Zener diodes)
  4. Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT)
  5. JFET; 6. IGFET/MOSFET
  7. [Thyristors](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/semiconductors/chpt-7/silicon-controlled-rectifier-scr/) (SCR, [TRIAC](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/semiconductors/chpt-7/the-triac/), DIAC)
  8. Operačné zosilňovače
- [Digital Circuits](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/digital/) — 13 kapít: číslované sústavy, binárna aritmetika, [logic gates](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/digital/chpt-3/digital-signals-gates/), Booleova algebra, Karnaughove mapy, latches a flip-flop, [counters](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/digital/chpt-11/asynchronous-counters/), DAC/ADC

## Ako použiť na maturitu

- T05 polovodiče: zväzok Semiconductors — PN priechod, diódy, usmerňovače, tyristory, TRIAC, tranzistor — presne zoznam okruhu.
- T01 číslicová technika: zväzok Digital Circuits — číslicové sústavy, logické brány, Booleova algebra, klopné obvody a počítadla.
- T02 digitalizácia: kapitola DAC/ADC.
- T03–T04 prvky R, L, C v obvode: zväzok DC, kap. 13–16 (kondenzátory, induktory).
