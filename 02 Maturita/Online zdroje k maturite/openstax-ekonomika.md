# Principles of Economics 2e (OpenStax)

> [!info] Originál
> [openstax.org/details/books/principles-economics](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-economics) · [web knihy](https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-2e/pages/index) — obsah prevzatý november 2026, pozri pôvodný web pre aktuálne znenie.

Bezplatná peer-reviewed univerzitná učebnica (Rice University), licencovaná CC BY-NC-SA. Online, v PDF aj tlačenej verzii. 34 kapitol: mikroekonomia aj makroekonomia, plus 4 prílohy.

## Obsah (skrátený zoznam kapitol)

- 1 What Is Economics; 2 Choice, PPF
- 3 [Demand and Supply](https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-2e/pages/3-1-demand-supply-and-equilibrium-in-markets-for-goods-and-services); 3.4 Price Ceilings and Floors
- 5 Elasticity; 6 Consumer Choices
- 7 [Production, Costs, and Industry Structure](https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-2e/pages/7-1-explicit-and-implicit-costs-and-accounting-and-economic-profit)
- 8 Perfect Competition; 9 Monopoly; 10 Monopolistic Competition and Oligopoly; 11 Antitrust
- 14 Labor Markets and Income; 17 [Financial Markets](https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-2e/pages/17-1-how-businesses-raise-financial-capital)
- 19 GDP; 21 Unemployment; 22 [Inflation](https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-2e/pages/22-1-tracking-inflation)
- 24 AD/AS model; 27 Money and Banking; 28 Monetary Policy
- 33 International Trade; 34 Protectionism
- Prílohy: matematika, indiferenčné krivky, diskontovaná hodnota

## Ako použiť na maturitu

Kap. 7 a 8 k E11 (náklady a výnosy), kap. 3 + 9–11 k E14 a E13, kap. 27–28 k E12 a E18, kap. 14 k E22.

Slovenské právne témy (E01 — živnostenský zákon SR, E27 — DPH SR) kniha nepokrýva; ostávajú vo vypracovaných témach trezora.
