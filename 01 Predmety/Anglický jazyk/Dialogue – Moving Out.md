# Dialogue – Moving Out

> [!info] Zdrojový dokument
> `Dialog - Tomas, Richard, Matt.docx` — [[Dialog - Tomas, Richard, Matt.docx|otvoriť originál]]

1. Richard: Please, dad, I’m ready to move out, can I do it?
1. Matt: Yes, you can, but why?
1. Tomas (with 1s delay): And why would you do that when you have everything here?
1. Richard: First of all, I’m 18. This means I can live by myself. Plus I have a girlfriend to take care of.
1. Matt: You can take care of your girlfriend right here in your room.
1. Richard: Yes, but you don’t want to know what I’m doing with her.
1. Tomas: Snorting sugar?
1. Richard: No, I’m doing something else with her.
1. Matt: You’re broke. If you move out, we will drain your bank account.
1. Richard: And why would you do that?
1. Matt: Because we love you and it’s for your own good.
1. Tomas: And we have trust issues.
1. Richard: As far as I know, I have no withdrawal limit on my bank account, so I can take all my money and run.
1. Matt: Your brother Larry is in hospital and he’ll die soon. So if you don’t want Larry to die, you have to stay here, otherwise he’ll die.
1. Tomas: And we already withdrew all your money to our accounts.
1. Richard: (sad, walks away)
1. (meanwhile)
1. Matt: Why did you say that?
1. Tomas: Because I don’t care about him.
1. Matt: And why don't you care about him? It’s your son anyway.
1. Tomas: Because I don’t like him. And he’s adopted.
1. Matt: So let’s return him back.
1. Tomas: Or let him go away from us.
1. (few minutes later)
1. Matt and Tomas: Richard, we decided that you can move out.
1. Richard (very happy): yay!
1. Narrator (Matt): The money was never wired back.

the end xD
