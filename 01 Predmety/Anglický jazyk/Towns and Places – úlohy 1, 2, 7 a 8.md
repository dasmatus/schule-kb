## Discussion questions 1, 2, 7 a 8

Pred čítaním textu, diskusia v triede (s. 97–98):

1. Where do you live? What makes the place where you live special for you?
2. What advantages and disadvantages are there of living in towns?
7. Describe the place you have been to that you like most.
8. Which town or place in the world would you most like to visit one day? Why?

Pre úplnosť, v tom istom zozname sú aj otázky 3–6, 9 a 10 (výhody a nevýhody života na vidieku, činnosti v meste a na vidieku, najnebezpečnejšie miesta, nedostupné destinácie, *Wonders of the World*, backpacking).
