## Exercise 1 — The Young and Their World

Súvisí s [[A17 – Young People]].

**A. Fill in the missing words/phrases**

1. Adolescence is described as a stage when a person is biologically adult but not fully __________ emotionally.
2. During puberty, boys develop deeper voices and more __________.
3. Adolescents often feel __________ and look for security.
4. A first symptom of anorexia is __________ in secret.
5. Sufferers of bulimia __________ and then vomit everything.
6. Young people tend to identify with __________, such as sports players or rock stars.
7. Vandalism and violence based on __________ are frequent among some adolescents.
8. Parents are often seen as __________, which creates tension.

**B. Open question**

Explain in two sentences what is the generation gap between parents and teenagers.

## Exercise 3 — Multicultural Society & International Institutions
