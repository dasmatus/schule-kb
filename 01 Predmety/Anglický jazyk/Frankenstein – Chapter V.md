## Úryvok (začiatok kapitoly)

> It was on a dreary night of November that I beheld the accomplishment
> of my toils. […] I saw the dull yellow eye of the creature open; it
> breathed hard, and a convulsive motion agitated its limbs.

Victor opisuje, ako po takmer dvoch rokoch práce oživil stvorenie, zhrozil sa ho a utiekol z izby.

## Otázky na porozumenie

Odpovede sú zhrnutím príslušných miest v kapitole, nie samostatnou interpretáciou.

1. **How long did Victor Frankenstein work on creating the creature?**
   Nearly two years. (*I had worked hard for nearly two years, for the sole purpose of infusing life into an inanimate body.*)
2. **How does Victor react when the creature finally comes to life?**
   The beauty of the dream vanishes; he is filled with horror and disgust, cannot endure the sight of the being and rushes out of the room.
3. **What nightmare does Victor have after creating the creature?**
   He dreams that he embraces Elizabeth in Ingolstadt; her lips become livid, and he finds himself holding the corpse of his dead mother, with grave-worms crawling in her shroud.
4. **Who is Henry Clerval, and how does he help Victor?**
   Henry is Victor’s friend from home, just arrived in Ingolstadt by the Swiss diligence. His presence briefly calms Victor; later he is Victor’s only nurse during a nervous fever that lasts several months, and he conceals the extent of the illness from Victor’s father and Elizabeth.
5. **What does Victor discover when he returns to his room?**
   He dreads that the creature is still there and that Henry might see it. He opens the door by force, but the apartment and the bedroom are empty — the creature has fled.
6. **How does Victor’s health change after the creature disappears?**
   At first he is wildly overexcited (jumping, clapping, laughing), then he collapses in a fit. A nervous fever confines him for several months; he recovers only slowly, with relapses, and feels better in spring.
